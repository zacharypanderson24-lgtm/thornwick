# Surviving the first year

Use the original two homes. This opening balances food security with hunting, lumber and mining; it is the best balanced opening tested, not a proof of a global optimum.

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1, paused | Reach 18 usable wheat plots within 10 tiles of the farmhouse; set flax to zero. Connect a silo and one hunting lodge near reachable forest. |
| By day 30 | Connect a woodcutter lodge at a separate forest and mark at least 18 trees. Keep timber cutting more than seven tiles from the hunting lodge where possible. |
| By day 60 | Connect a sawmill. Keep food work ahead of industry in worker priorities. |
| Days 101-109 | First wheat normally arrives if both farmers sowed from day 1. Later sowing delays harvest. |
| Around day 120 | Connect a quarry, mark nearby stone for mining, and build an iron mine against a suitable hill face with its entrance on a connected road. Survey ore, require supports and allow at most the first shaft level. |
| Autumn and winter | Keep hunting, remark timber as trees run out, and retain food for the next spring. Avoid extra housing, animals and recruitment until supplies can support them. |
| Day 361 | Aim for all five industries connected and at least 100 days of edible food stored. |

Roads and buildings must be affordable and connected. A mine sprite alone does not count: check actual ore deliveries. A quarry supplies stone, not iron ore. One farmer manages nine fields; wheat takes 100 days to grow. The first harvest cannot solve an immediate spring shortage.

## Verification

Full simulation runs used normal placement, zoning, road costs, worker allocation and daily ticks on seeds 7, 42 and 123. No resources were injected. Each run completed 360 days with 16 villagers, no starvation or water shortage, no miner deaths, and delivered timber, stone and ore. Final edible food was approximately 505-561, with minimum reserves approximately 85-110. Campaign settlements and camps were not attached to these tests, so these results do not establish safety against every campaign raid or every terrain seed.

The regression tests also drive the tutorial's world goals through these openings. Camera and panel signals are supplied by the harness. Compilation and simulation checks do not replace a full Unity Play Mode UI walkthrough.
